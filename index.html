Formulas & assumptions

Ph = ρ · g · Q · H / 3.6×10⁶ [kW], g = 9.81 m/s²

ηduty = ηBEP · r(2 − r), r = Q / QBEP — parabolic part-load approximation

Pshaft = Ph / ηduty · Pin = Ph / (ηduty · ηmotor)

Eyr = Pin · hours · tariff · TCO = CapEx + n·Eyr + n·Maint (undiscounted, constant tariff)

CO₂ = Pin · hours · EF / 1000 [t/yr]

Driver check (API 610): nameplate ≥ Pshaft × 1.25 (<22 kW) / 1.15 (22–55 kW) / 1.10 (>55 kW)

Rated flow 80–110% of BEP (minor), outside 70–120% (major) · NPSH margin ≥ 1.0 m · Material class ranking simplified from API 610 Annex H

Recommendation = lowest TCO among bids with no major deviation. Commercial flags do not affect compliance.

Text is processed in this tab. With the local parser nothing leaves your browser. With a cloud provider, the text is sent directly from your browser to the endpoint you configure, under your account and that provider's data terms.

Extraction engine

Deterministic regular-expression parser. Recognises common quotation phrasing for price, BEP efficiency and flow, NPSH3/NPSHr, MAWP, material class, motor rating and efficiency, maintenance allowance and lead time. No key required.

Unpaid Gemini API tiers may use submitted content to improve Google products. Use a paid key or the local parser for confidential pricing.
