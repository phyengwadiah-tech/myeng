Deterministic physics

All calculations are plain JavaScript in your browser. No MYENG server receives your inputs.

Azure AI Foundry & Gemini, your key

Models only extract fields from quotation text. Requests go from your browser to your endpoint, and every value is cross-checked against a local parser.

No MYENG backend

Projects stay in this browser’s local storage. Text you send to Azure or Gemini follows that provider’s retention terms.

What we do not claim

  • MYENG is not certified, accredited or endorsed by API, ISO or any standards body.
  • It checks a simplified subset of API 610 criteria and does not verify vendor data, test results or mechanical design.
  • Efficiency at duty uses a parabolic approximation; lifecycle cost is undiscounted at a constant tariff.
  • Every output needs review by a qualified engineer before award.

    Formulas & assumptions

    Ph = ρ · g · Q · H / 3.6×10⁶ [kW], g = 9.81 m/s²

    ηduty = ηBEP · r(2 − r), r = Q / QBEP — parabolic part-load approximation

    Pshaft = Ph / ηduty · Pin = Ph / (ηduty · ηmotor)

    Eyr = Pin · hours · tariff · TCO = CapEx + n·Eyr + n·Maint (undiscounted, constant tariff)

    CO₂ = Pin · hours · EF / 1000 [t/yr]

    Driver check (API 610): nameplate ≥ Pshaft × 1.25 (<22 kW) / 1.15 (22–55 kW) / 1.10 (>55 kW)

    Rated flow 80–110% of BEP (minor), outside 70–120% (major) · NPSH margin ≥ 1.0 m · Material class ranking simplified from API 610 Annex H

    Recommendation = lowest TCO among bids with no major deviation. Commercial flags do not affect compliance.
